# Member interviews — raw notes, September 2026

Hazel Street Co-op (fictional). Interviews by Inès (UX research), 8 members, 40 to 60 min, in the back room of the store or by phone. Names replaced by P1–P8. Notes taken during the interview, quotes word for word, cleaned up the same day.

Recruited from the volunteers list of the newsletter, 2026-09-01: we asked for "members who find the shifts easy, and members who find them hard". Nobody who left the co-op.

Reminder of the rules (as the members office states them): every member works one 3-hour shift every 4 weeks, always in the same slot (e.g. "Tuesday 9–12, week B"). A missed shift = 1 make-up shift owed. 2 make-up shifts owed = suspended: cannot shop until they are done. A swap must be sent to the members office by email, at least 48 h before.

---

## P1 — 2026-09-08, phone, 50 min

Nurse at the hospital, rotating rota, member for 2 years. Suspended twice.

- Gets her rota 2 weeks ahead, sometimes changed the week before. "My Tuesday morning is a Tuesday morning one month out of two." "I'm already stressed when the rota comes out."
- Missed 3 shifts this year, always because of the rota. Each time she tried to swap first.
- Swaps: posts in the WhatsApp group of her team. "Sometimes someone answers in 10 minutes, sometimes nobody at all." "I hate asking, I feel like I'm begging."
- Never knows how many make-ups she owes. "I count in my head and I'm always wrong."
- Second suspension: found out at the checkout, with a full basket. "The cashier was nice but I wanted to disappear."
- Would like to "throw the shift to anyone who can take it", not find one person for one exact trade.

## P2 — 2026-09-09, back room, 45 min

Retired teacher, member since the opening (2021). Never missed a shift. Does 2 or 3 extra shifts per cycle.

- Has her shifts on a paper calendar on the fridge. Does not use the member space ("my grandson set it up once").
- Watches the whiteboard at the entrance ("shifts that need people") and writes her name. "If nobody takes the Saturday evening, the store doesn't open the cheese counter."
- The best part of the co-op: "the people. You fold boxes with a lawyer and a plumber."
- About the young ones: "They don't understand that missing a shift means someone else does two."
- Would not install an app. "If it's only on the phone, count me out."

## P3 — 2026-09-10, back room, 40 min

Student, member for 4 months.

- Missed her second shift (exams), did not know she had to do a make-up. "Nobody told me there was a debt."
- Missed another one in July (holiday). Learned at the checkout that she was suspended. "I thought it was a bug."
- The onboarding session: "2 hours, lots of rules, I remember the coffee."
- Looked for her status in the member space: found a page "Status: on alert" with no explanation. "On alert of what?"
- Uses Instagram and WhatsApp, not email. Did not see the reminder emails (in the promotions tab).

## P4 — 2026-09-11, phone, 45 min

Father of two children under 5, works full time, member for 18 months.

- Swaps "almost every other cycle": a child sick, a school event.
- Only uses the WhatsApp group of his team. "The email to the office is a formality, I do it after."
- Sometimes forgets to email the office: the swap is then counted as a missed shift. Happened once, "I had to argue for 3 emails. I was furious, I had found someone!"
- When he misses one: "I feel guilty towards the team, they're one short."
- Would like a reminder 2 or 3 days before, "not a week before, I don't know my week yet".
- Values the co-op for the prices and "knowing where the food comes from".

## P5 — 2026-09-15, phone, 55 min

Freelance graphic designer, irregular work, member for 1 year.

- Swaps often. Finds the team group too small: "8 people, half of them never answer."
- Has written to the members office for a swap: answer after 4 days, the shift was already passed. "So I was absent, officially."
- Would give her shift to "anyone in the co-op", not only her team.
- Wants to see the open shifts of the whole co-op to do her make-up "when I have a hole in my week".
- Considered leaving in the spring. Stayed "because of the prices and because I'd feel bad".

## P6 — 2026-09-16, back room, 40 min

New member, 6 weeks. Moved to the area in July.

- Onboarding session in August. Remembers "the 3 hours every 4 weeks" but not the slot system.
- Could not find his shift calendar in the member space for 2 weeks (it is under "My account > Participation").
- Asked the cashier during his first shopping how shifts work. "She explained better in 2 minutes than the session."
- Wanted to choose his slot. Was given "Thursday 18–21, week D" by the office, which does not suit him. Did not know he could ask to change.

## P7 — 2026-09-17, phone, 50 min

Works nights in logistics, member for 2 years.

- Often free on weekday mornings at short notice. "I'd do a shift tomorrow morning if someone told me there's a gap."
- Does his make-ups by turning up and asking at the shift desk. "Sometimes they don't need anyone, so I go home. Annoying, I came for nothing."
- Missed 2 shifts after a change of his work schedule; was suspended for 10 days.
- Had to email the office 3 times to change his slot. Took 5 weeks.

## P8 — 2026-09-18, back room, 60 min

Member for 3 years, was on the board in 2023. Thinking about leaving.

- "The co-op is a great idea run with a 2010 spreadsheet."
- His shift slot is fixed and suits him; never suspended.
- Frustrated for others: "We lose people who'd stay if the rules bent a little."
- Knows 4 members who left in the last year; "3 of them over the shifts", says he.
- Values the store "as a place where people talk to each other", and the vote at the general assembly.
- Suggests a "pause" for parental leave or long trips. Today, the office decides case by case.

## First impressions (Inès, 2026-09-19)

- Missing a shift is about a week that changes, not about not caring (P1, P4, P5, P7).
- Nobody knows their status for sure (P1, P3); learning it at the checkout is the worst moment (P1, P3).
- Swaps live in WhatsApp; the office is a step after, or too late (P1, P4, P5).
- The ones who never miss are the ones with a stable week (P2, P8). Small sample: all of them volunteered.
