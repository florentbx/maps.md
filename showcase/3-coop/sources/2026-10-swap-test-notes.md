# Usability test of the swap prototype — notes, October 2026

Hazel Street Co-op (fictional). Moderated test, think-aloud, 30 min, in the back room, on a phone (Figma prototype v1 of the member app). Inès moderates, Malik (designer) takes notes. 6 participants recruited at the store: T1–T6, none of them interviewed in September.

Dates: 2026-10-20 (T1–T3), 2026-10-22 (T4–T6).

Participants: T1 member 3 years, never swaps · T2 member 8 months, swaps often · T3 member 2 months · T4 member 1 year, parent · T5 member 4 years, 68, uses a phone "for calls and photos" · T6 member 6 months, student.

The prototype: a home screen with "Your next shift" and "Your status"; a "Swap" button on the shift, which asks to choose a member of the team and a date; a list "Open shifts" for make-ups. The swap follows the decision of the app team of 2026-09-25: one-for-one swaps within the team, as the rules say today.

## Task 1 — When is your next shift, and are you up to date?

- 6/6 found the next shift in under 10 s.
- Status "On alert": 4/6 did not understand it (T2, T3, T5, T6). T3: "Alert? Did I do something wrong?" T6: "Is it about the stock?"
- 5/6 looked for a number of shifts owed. T4: "Just tell me: you owe 1 shift."

## Task 2 — You cannot make your next shift: arrange it

- 3/6 succeeded (T1, T2, T4), between 1 and 3 minutes.
- T3, T5, T6 stopped at "choose a member": "I don't know who's free. That's the whole problem." (T6)
- 4/6 looked first for a button "I can't come" or "give away" (T2, T3, T4, T6). T2: "I just want to put it out there and let someone grab it."
- T1 and T4 asked whether the office must approve the swap. Nobody knew.
- T4: wants to be told when someone has taken the shift. "Otherwise I'll check every hour."

## Task 3 — You owe a make-up: find one

- 5/6 succeeded (all but T5). The list of open shifts with "2 spots left" was liked: "Like the whiteboard but at home." (T2)
- T5 gave up: text too small, "I'll go to the whiteboard".

## After the test (Inès and Malik, 2026-10-22)

- Status: words, not a colour or "On alert". A number of shifts owed.
- The swap as designed (one-for-one, with a chosen member) fails for those who don't know who is free. Next version: a pool of shifts to give away, open to all members.
- T5: the app will not replace the whiteboard for everybody (see survey Q12).
