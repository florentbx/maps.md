# Team meeting — research read-out, 2026-10-24

Hazel Street Co-op (fictional). Present: Inès (UX research), Malik (design), Jo and Sam (the volunteers who build the member app), Claire (members office). Notes by Malik.

## Read-out

Inès presented the 5 studies (interviews, survey, store, swap test, office mailbox) and the insights. Main points discussed: the status nobody understands, the swap that fails when you must pick someone, the status that lags because of the paper sheets.

## Decided

- The app shows the status as the number of make-up shifts owed, in plain words ("You owe 1 make-up shift"), no more "On alert". Claire: the office will use the same words in its emails. The tablet at the checkout: to see with the board, it is not ours.
- The swap of prototype v1 (one-for-one, choose a member of the team, decided 2026-09-25) is dropped. v2: "I can't come" puts the shift in a pool open to all members; anyone can take it; the giver is told when it is taken. Jo raised a third option, "share to my team's WhatsApp group" from the app: kept as a fallback, not built first.
- The whiteboard and the shift desk stay. The app is one more way, not the only one (survey Q12, T5).

## Not decided

- Must the office still approve a swap? Today the rules say yes (48 h, by email). With the pool, Claire is for "no approval, the app records it". It changes the rules of the co-op: to propose at the general assembly of 2026-12-13.
- What may a cashier do when a member is suspended? Not for the app team: Claire takes it to the board.

## Next studies (Inès)

- With Claire: compare the paper sheets of October with the status in the system, to measure the lag. November.
- Interview members who left: Claire asks the board whether we may contact them. Not before December.
