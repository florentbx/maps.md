# Member survey — results, September 2026

Hazel Street Co-op (fictional). Online survey (form sent by the newsletter, by email), open from 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-19, by Inès. 412 answers out of 1,880 active members (22%). No incentive. Members who left the co-op were not reached.

## Missed shifts

Q4. In the last 6 months, have you missed at least one shift (without a swap)?

| Answer | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| Yes | 157 | 38% |
| No | 255 | 62% |

Q5. (If yes) Why did you miss your last shift? One answer.

| Answer | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| My work or family schedule changed | 64 | 41% |
| I forgot | 42 | 27% |
| I was ill | 28 | 18% |
| I could not find anyone to swap with | 14 | 9% |
| Other | 9 | 5% |

## Swaps

Q7. How do you usually arrange a swap?
