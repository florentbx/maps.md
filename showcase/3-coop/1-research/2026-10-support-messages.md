## Context
Goal: see what members need from the members office, and what the office cannot answer in time.

- One main tag per thread, by Inès, on 2026-10-16; the figures of the office are theirs, of the same day.
- The office: 2 volunteers, 4 hours a week each.
- No participants named: the evidence is figures and messages, word for word.
- Source: [[2026-10-support-messages-export]].

## Swaps and status are most of the messages
- Description: Swaps come first, then questions on the status; together with make-ups they are three quarters of the threads.
- Theme: What members ask
- Evidence:
  - Swap 36%, status 26%, make-up shifts 14%, change of slot 12%, pause 8%, other 5% (146 threads)

## Swaps are announced too late for the office
- Description: Swap messages are often sent at the last moment, and the office answers days later; more than a third of them are answered after the shift.
- Theme: Swapping
- Evidence:
  - Median first answer: 3 days (a few hours to 12 days)
  - 19 of 52 swap threads answered after the date of the shift
  - "Hi, I can't do Tuesday, Marie from my team takes it. Sorry for the late notice!" (the morning of the shift)

## Members suspended for a shift they did
- Description: More than a third of the questions on the status come from members who did their shift, not typed in yet; a quarter ask what "on alert" means.
- Theme: Status
- Evidence:
  - 14 of 38 status threads: the shift was done, not typed in yet (checked by the office)
  - 9 of 38 ask what "on alert" means
  - "Why am I suspended?? I came on the 3rd, ask Paul he was the leader."

## Members ask for a pause, and get suspended
- Description: Members facing a birth, a long trip or an illness ask how to stop their shifts for a while; there is no rule, the office decides case by case.
- Theme: Life events
- Evidence:
  - 11 of 146 threads (8%)
  - "I'm having a baby in December, how do I stop the shifts for a while without being suspended?"

## Changing slot takes several emails
- Description: Members who ask to change their slot or their team write several times before an answer.
- Theme: What members ask
- Evidence:
  - 17 of 146 threads (12%)
  - "I've asked twice to change to the weekend. Is anyone reading this?"

## One member in fourteen is suspended
- Description: The office counts 7% of the active members suspended on 2026-10-01, and 214 members who left in a year, most without a reason given.
- Theme: Figures of the office
- Evidence:
  - 131 of 1,880 active members suspended on 2026-10-01 (7%)
  - 214 left in 12 months; no reason given for 160 of them
