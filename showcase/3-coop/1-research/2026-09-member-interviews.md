## Context
Goal: understand why members miss their shifts and get suspended, and how they arrange their shifts today.

- Questions: how do members plan their shift; what happens when they cannot come; how do they know where they stand.
- Scope: members today; the members who left are out of scope (none was recruited).
- Participants: P1 nurse on a rotating rota, 2 years; P2 retired, since 2021; P3 student, 4 months; P4 parent of two, 18 months; P5 freelance, 1 year; P6 new member, 6 weeks; P7 night worker, 2 years; P8 former board member, 3 years.
- Source: [[2026-09-interview-notes]].

## A shift is missed when the week changes
- Description: The members who missed shifts all name a change in their week: a new rota, a sick child, a work schedule moved. They tried to swap first, and missed the shift when nobody took it.
- Theme: Missing a shift
- Participants:
  - P1
  - P4
  - P5
  - P7
- Evidence:
  - "My Tuesday morning is a Tuesday morning one month out of two."
    - Participant: P1
  - Missed 2 shifts after a change of his work schedule, suspended for 10 days
    - Participant: P7
  - "I feel guilty towards the team, they're one short."
    - Participant: P4

## Members with a stable week never miss
- Description: The two members who never missed a shift have a week that does not move; one of them does extra shifts and fills the gaps of others.
- Theme: Missing a shift
- Participants:
  - P2
  - P8
- Evidence:
  - Does 2 or 3 extra shifts per cycle, from the whiteboard at the entrance
    - Participant: P2

## Swaps are arranged in the team's WhatsApp group
- Description: The members swap by posting in the WhatsApp group of their team; the email to the members office comes after, or is forgotten. A forgotten email turns the swap into a missed shift.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - P1
  - P4
  - P5
- Evidence:
  - "The email to the office is a formality, I do it after."
    - Participant: P4
  - Forgot the email once; the swap was counted as a missed shift, "I had to argue for 3 emails. I was furious, I had found someone!"
    - Participant: P4
  - "I hate asking, I feel like I'm begging."
    - Participant: P1

## The team is too small to find a swap
- Description: A team has about 8 members; when nobody answers, there is nobody else to ask. Members want to hand the shift to anyone in the co-op, not find one person for one exact trade.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - P1
  - P5
- Evidence:
  - "8 people, half of them never answer."
    - Participant: P5
  - Would like to "throw the shift to anyone who can take it".
    - Participant: P1

## The office answers after the shift
- Description: A swap or a change of slot asked by email to the members office gets its answer days or weeks later, sometimes after the shift has passed.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - P5
  - P7
- Evidence:
  - Answer after 4 days, the shift already passed, "So I was absent, officially."
    - Participant: P5
  - Emailed 3 times to change his slot; it took 5 weeks
    - Participant: P7

## Nobody knows how many make-ups they owe
- Description: The members count their make-up shifts in their head, or do not know there is anything to count; the member space shows "On alert" with no explanation.
- Theme: Status
- Participants:
  - P1
  - P3
- Evidence:
  - "I count in my head and I'm always wrong."
    - Participant: P1
  - "On alert of what?"
    - Participant: P3

## Suspension is learned at the checkout
- Description: Members learn that they are suspended at the checkout, with their basket, in front of the queue; they feel ashamed and think at first of a bug.
- Theme: Status
- Participants:
  - P1
  - P3
- Evidence:
  - "The cashier was nice but I wanted to disappear."
    - Participant: P1
  - "I thought it was a bug."
    - Participant: P3

## New members leave the onboarding without the rules
- Description: The new members remember "3 hours every 4 weeks" from the onboarding session, not the make-up rule nor where to find their shifts; a cashier or a shift leader explains it later.
- Theme: Joining
- Participants:
  - P3
  - P6
- Evidence:
  - "Nobody told me there was a debt."
    - Participant: P3
  - Could not find his shift calendar for 2 weeks; "She explained better in 2 minutes than the session."
    - Participant: P6

## Some members want shifts at short notice
- Description: Members with free time that comes at short notice would fill a gap the next day, or do their make-up then, if they knew where a team is short; turning up at the desk does not always work.
- Theme: Make-up shifts
- Participants:
  - P5
  - P7
- Evidence:
  - "I'd do a shift tomorrow morning if someone told me there's a gap."
    - Participant: P7
  - Turns up for a make-up, "Sometimes they don't need anyone, so I go home. Annoying, I came for nothing."
    - Participant: P7

## Working together is what members value
- Description: Members value the shift as a moment with people they would not meet otherwise, and the store as a place where people talk; they fear an app that would replace it.
- Theme: What members value
- Participants:
  - P2
  - P8
- Evidence:
  - "You fold boxes with a lawyer and a plumber."
    - Participant: P2

## The rules push some members out
- Description: Two members talk of leaving, or of others who left, because of the rigidity of the shifts; they stay for the prices, the people or out of guilt. One asks for a pause for life events.
- Theme: What members value
- Participants:
  - P5
  - P8
- Evidence:
  - "We lose people who'd stay if the rules bent a little."
    - Participant: P8
  - Considered leaving in the spring; stayed "because of the prices and because I'd feel bad".
    - Participant: P5
