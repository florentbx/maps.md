## Context
Goal: can members see where they stand, give up a shift and find a make-up in the first prototype of the member app?

- Tasks: 1. When is your next shift, and are you up to date? 2. You cannot make your next shift: arrange it. 3. You owe a make-up: find one.
- The prototype: a home with "Your next shift" and "Your status"; a "Swap" button that asks to choose a member of the team and a date; a list "Open shifts".
- Participants: T1 3 years, never swaps; T2 8 months, swaps often; T3 2 months; T4 1 year, parent; T5 4 years, 68; T6 6 months, student. None of them was interviewed in September.
- Sessions on 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-22.
- Source: [[2026-10-swap-test-notes]].

## "On alert" is not understood
- Description: The next shift was found by all at once; the status "On alert" was not understood by most, who read it as a fault or something else. Most looked for a number of shifts owed.
- Theme: Status
- Participants:
  - T2
  - T3
  - T5
  - T6
- Evidence:
  - "Alert? Did I do something wrong?"
    - Participant: T3
  - "Is it about the stock?"
    - Participant: T6
  - "Just tell me, you owe 1 shift."
    - Participant: T4

## Choosing a member to swap with blocks the swap
- Description: Half of the participants could not swap: they stopped when asked to choose a member, as they do not know who is free.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - T3
  - T5
  - T6
- Evidence:
  - "I don't know who's free. That's the whole problem."
    - Participant: T6
  - 3 of 6 succeeded, in 1 to 3 minutes (T1, T2, T4)

## Members look for "I can't come", not "Swap"
- Description: Most participants looked first for a way to give the shift away, to put it out there for anyone to take.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - T2
  - T3
  - T4
  - T6
- Evidence:
  - "I just want to put it out there and let someone grab it."
    - Participant: T2

## Nobody knows if the office approves a swap
- Description: Two participants asked whether the members office must approve a swap; nobody knew.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - T1
  - T4
- Evidence:
  - Asked during task 2
    - Participant: T1

## Members want to know when their shift is taken
- Description: A participant who gave a shift away wanted to be told when someone took it, rather than check.
- Theme: Swapping
- Participants:
  - T4
- Evidence:
  - "Otherwise I'll check every hour."
    - Participant: T4

## The list of open shifts works
- Description: Almost all found a make-up shift in the list of open shifts, and liked seeing how many spots are left; the oldest participant gave up, the text too small, and would go to the whiteboard.
- Theme: Make-up shifts
- Participants:
  - T1
  - T2
  - T3
  - T4
  - T6
- Evidence:
  - "Like the whiteboard but at home."
    - Participant: T2
  - Gave up, text too small, "I'll go to the whiteboard".
    - Participant: T5
