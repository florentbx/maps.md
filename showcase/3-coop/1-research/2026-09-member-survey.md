## Context
Goal: measure what the interviews heard: how often shifts are missed and why, how swaps are arranged, whether members know their status, and whether they would use an app.

- Open from 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-19; no incentive.
- Bias: answered by members who read the newsletter, probably fewer of those already disengaged; the members who left were not reached.
- No participants named: the evidence is figures, with their question.
- Source: [[2026-09-survey-results]].

## A changed schedule is the first reason to miss a shift
- Description: 38% of the members missed at least one shift in 6 months; for their last one, a change of schedule comes first, before forgetting and illness. Not finding a swap is named by few, as a reason in itself.
- Theme: Missing a shift
- Evidence:
  - 157 of 412 (38%) missed at least one shift in the last 6 months (Q4)
  - Last shift missed: schedule changed 41%, forgot 27%, ill 18%, no swap found 9% (Q5, n = 157)

## Most swaps go through WhatsApp
- Description: More than half of the members arrange their swaps in the WhatsApp group of their team; one in five by email to the office.
- Theme: Swapping
- Evidence:
  - WhatsApp group 58%, email to the office 21%, at the store 12%, never swapped 9% (Q7)
  - "Let us swap with anyone, not only our team." (Q16)

## Half of the members do not know their status
- Description: Fewer than half of the members know how many make-up shifts they owe.
- Theme: Status
- Evidence:
  - Know how many make-ups they owe: yes 46%, no 31%, not sure 23% (Q9)
  - "A clear counter: you owe 1 shift. That's all I need." (Q16)

## One member in seven was stopped at the checkout
- Description: 14% of the members have learned at the checkout that they could not shop.
- Theme: Status
- Evidence:
  - 58 of 412 (14%) learned at the checkout that they could not shop (Q10)
  - "I got suspended for a shift I DID. The sheet was typed a week later." (Q16)

## Most members would use an app, not all
- Description: 71% would use a phone app to see and swap their shifts; 12% would not, mostly members of 65 or older.
- Theme: A member app
- Evidence:
  - Yes 71%, maybe 17%, no 12% (Q12)
  - 31 of the 49 "no" are 65 or older (Q12, Q2)
  - "Please don't turn it into an app where we never see each other." (Q16)

## Members like the co-op, not the shifts
- Description: The members rate being a member high, and the organisation of the shifts low.
- Theme: What members value
- Evidence:
  - Being a member: 4.1 of 5; how the shifts are organised: 2.6 of 5 (Q14, Q15)
  - "I love the store, I hate the spreadsheet of shifts." (Q16)
