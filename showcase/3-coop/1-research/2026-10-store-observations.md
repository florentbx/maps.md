## Context
Goal: see what happens at the checkout when a member's status is checked, and how a shift done becomes a status.

- Sessions: 2026-10-06, 10, 14 and 17, weekday evenings, mornings and Saturdays.
- No participants named: the evidence is what was seen, with its session.
- Source: [[2026-10-store-observations-notes]].

## Suspended members are stopped at the checkout, surprised
- Description: 9 of 214 members were shown suspended on the tablet; 6 of them did not know. Most left their basket or had a friend pay; the queue waited several minutes each time.
- Theme: At the checkout
- Evidence:
  - 9 of 214 checkouts (4%) shown "Suspended"; 6 of the 9 surprised
  - 4 left their basket, 2 had a friend in the queue pay; 3 to 6 minutes of waiting each time

## Cashiers apply the rule differently
- Description: Some cashiers let a suspended member pay "this time", others do not; two gave opposite answers to the same question about a member on alert.
- Theme: At the checkout
- Evidence:
  - 3 of the 9 suspended members let through by the cashier
  - "Can she pay if she's on alert?": yes on 2026-10-06, "I think not" on 2026-10-17

## The status lags behind the shifts done
- Description: Attendance is written on paper at the shift desk and typed in by a volunteer once a week; a member who did her shift was shown suspended because her sheet was not typed yet.
- Theme: Attendance
- Evidence:
  - The box of sheets on 2026-10-14 held sheets back to 2026-10-03
  - On 2026-10-10, a member shown suspended had her name on the sheet of her shift

## Swaps are known by the shift leaders, not the office
- Description: A shift leader keeps her own notebook of who swapped with whom, because the office does not know.
- Theme: Attendance
- Evidence:
  - "The office never knows." (shift leader, 2026-10-17)

## The whiteboard fills only half of the gaps
- Description: The board of shifts that need people, by the entrance, is used: members write their names by hand. About half of its lines get a name.
- Theme: Make-up shifts
- Evidence:
  - 5 to 8 lines each session; 3 of 6 filled on 2026-10-17
  - Of 2 members who came for a make-up unannounced, one was sent home (2026-10-14)

## New members ask how it works in the store
- Description: New members ask the shift leader or the cashier how the shifts work.
- Theme: Joining
- Evidence:
  - 7 times in 8 hours

## The shift is a social moment
- Description: At every session, the members on shift talk while they work: jokes, recipes, the news of the neighbourhood.
- Theme: What members value
- Evidence:
  - Seen at all 4 sessions
