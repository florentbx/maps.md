## Context
Drawn from [[insights]] and the studies behind it, by Inès, 2026-10. A persona is a way of doing: a member can move from one to another when their life changes.

- The quotes are those of the participants, with their code (P for the interviews, T for the test).
- How many members are like each persona is not known: the survey did not ask it ([[research-questions#How many members are like each persona?]]).

## The member whose week moves
- Description: A member with a job or a family that changes their week from one month to the next: a rota, young children, freelance work ([[insights#Members miss a shift when their week changes]]).
- Goals:
  - Keep the shift when the week allows, hand it on when it does not
  - Know at any time whether they can shop
- Frustrations:
  - Nobody in the team answers in time ([[insights#Members want to give a shift away, not trade it]])
  - The office answers after the shift ([[insights#The members office is too slow for a swap]])
  - Learns of a suspension at the checkout ([[insights#Members learn they are suspended at the checkout]])
- Behaviours:
  - Posts in the WhatsApp group of the team, emails the office after, or forgets to
  - Counts the make-up shifts owed in their head
  - Turns up at the shift desk for a make-up when a morning is free
- Context of use:
  - On the phone, in the evening or between two shifts at work, a few days before the shift
- Quotes:
  - "My Tuesday morning is a Tuesday morning one month out of two." (P1)
  - "8 people, half of them never answer." (P5)
  - "I just want to put it out there and let someone grab it." (T2)

## The member who holds the store together
- Description: A member with a stable week, often retired or with a fixed job, who never misses and fills the gaps of others ([[insights#A stable week makes a reliable member]]).
- Goals:
  - Keep the store open and the teams full
  - Share the work fairly
- Frustrations:
  - Others who miss their shift, so that someone does two
  - An organisation that may move to a phone they do not use ([[insights#Not every member will use a phone app]])
- Behaviours:
  - Keeps the shifts on a paper calendar
  - Reads the whiteboard at the entrance and writes their name on the gaps
- Context of use:
  - In the store, at the whiteboard and the shift desk; a phone for calls, not for apps
- Quotes:
  - "If nobody takes the Saturday evening, the store doesn't open the cheese counter." (P2)
  - "If it's only on the phone, count me out." (P2)
  - "You fold boxes with a lawyer and a plumber." (P2)

## The new member who does not know the rules yet
- Description: A member of a few weeks or months, who left the onboarding with "3 hours every 4 weeks" and little else ([[insights#New members leave the onboarding without the rules]]).
- Goals:
  - Understand what is expected, and do it right
  - Find their shift without asking
- Frustrations:
  - Did not know a missed shift becomes a debt
  - "On alert" with no explanation ([[insights#Members do not know where they stand]])
  - A slot given by the office that does not suit them, without knowing it can change
- Behaviours:
  - Asks the cashier or the shift leader how it works
  - Reads messages on Instagram and WhatsApp, not the emails of the co-op
- Context of use:
  - On the phone; in the store during the first shifts and shopping trips
- Quotes:
  - "Nobody told me there was a debt." (P3)
  - "On alert of what?" (P3)
  - "Alert? Did I do something wrong?" (T3)
