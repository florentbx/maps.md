## Context
The journey of [[personas#The member whose week moves]], as it is today, from the interviews of September and the studies of October; the quotes are word for word, from [[2026-09-interview-notes]] unless said otherwise.

- Scope: from the week that changes to coming back to the store after a suspension.
- The emotions are those the participants said; elsewhere `undefined`.
- The opportunities are those the team decided or the members asked for ([[opportunity-tree]], [[decisions]]).

## The week changes
- Description: A new rota, a sick child or a new schedule falls on the day of the shift.
- Actions:
  - Gets the new rota, or the news
  - Sees the clash with the fixed slot
- Touchpoints:
  - Work rota
  - Calendar
- Thoughts:
  - "My Tuesday morning is a Tuesday morning one month out of two."
- Emotion: -1, stressed
- Pain points:
  - The slot is fixed, the week is not
- Insights:
  - [[insights#Members miss a shift when their week changes]]
- Opportunities:
  - A reminder 2 or 3 days before the shift
    - Description: Asked by a parent: a week ahead, he does not know his week yet.

## Look for a swap
- Description: The member asks the WhatsApp group of the team who can take the shift.
- Actions:
  - Posts in the team's WhatsApp group
  - Waits for an answer
- Touchpoints:
  - WhatsApp group of the team
- Thoughts:
  - "Sometimes someone answers in 10 minutes, sometimes nobody at all."
  - "I hate asking, I feel like I'm begging."
- Emotion: -1, begging
- Pain points:
  - Nobody in the team answers in time
  - One person must be found for one exact trade
- Insights:
  - [[insights#Swaps live in WhatsApp, outside the co-op's tools]]
  - [[insights#Members want to give a shift away, not trade it]]
- Opportunities:
  - A pool of shifts to give away, open to all members
    - Description: "I can't come" puts the shift in a pool; anyone can take it; the giver is told when it is taken.
    - Status: planned
    - Owner: the member app team

## Tell the office
- Description: Once a swap is found, the member emails the members office, as the rules ask, at least 48 hours before.
- Actions:
  - Emails the office, after the swap or the morning of the shift
  - Forgets, sometimes
- Touchpoints:
  - Email to the members office
- Thoughts:
  - "The email to the office is a formality, I do it after."
- Emotion: -2, furious
- Pain points:
  - The answer comes after the shift
    - Evidence: 19 of 52 swap emails answered after the date of the shift
  - A forgotten email turns the swap into a missed shift
- Insights:
  - [[insights#The members office is too slow for a swap]]
- Opportunities:
  - A swap recorded by the app, with no approval of the office
    - Description: Proposed at the general assembly of 2026-12-13.

## Miss the shift
- Description: Nobody took the shift, or the office did not record the swap: the member is marked absent.
- Actions:
  - Does not come
- Touchpoints:
  - Paper sheet of the shift
- Thoughts:
  - "I feel guilty towards the team, they're one short."
- Emotion: -2, guilty
- Pain points:
  - The team works one short
- Insights:
  - [[insights#Members miss a shift when their week changes]]
- Opportunities: undefined

## Owe a make-up
- Description: The member owes a make-up shift, and must find one before a second miss suspends them.
- Actions:
  - Counts the shifts owed
  - Turns up at the shift desk, or reads the whiteboard
- Touchpoints:
  - Member space
  - Whiteboard
  - Shift desk
- Thoughts:
  - "I count in my head and I'm always wrong."
  - "Sometimes they don't need anyone, so I go home."
- Emotion: -1, annoyed
- Pain points:
  - Does not know how many shifts are owed
  - Sent home when no team is short
- Insights:
  - [[insights#Members do not know where they stand]]
  - [[insights#Open shifts would be filled if members saw them]]
- Opportunities:
  - The number of make-up shifts owed, in plain words
    - Status: planned
    - Owner: the member app team
  - The open shifts of the whole co-op, with the spots left
    - Status: planned
    - Owner: the member app team

## Stopped at the checkout
- Description: The member comes to shop; the tablet of the cashier shows them suspended.
- Actions:
  - Comes to shop with a full basket
  - Leaves the basket, or a friend pays
- Touchpoints:
  - Tablet at the checkout
  - Cashier
- Thoughts:
  - "The cashier was nice but I wanted to disappear."
  - "I thought it was a bug."
- Emotion: -3, ashamed
- Pain points:
  - Learns the suspension in front of the queue
  - Suspended for a shift done, not typed in yet
  - The cashier decides alone whether they may pay
- Insights:
  - [[insights#Members learn they are suspended at the checkout]]
  - [[insights#The status is wrong for days after a shift]]
  - [[insights#Cashiers decide alone whether a suspended member pays]]
- Opportunities: undefined
