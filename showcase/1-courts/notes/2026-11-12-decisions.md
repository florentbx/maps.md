# Decisions, with Hugo

*Fictional notes, written for the showcase of maps.md.*

2026-11-12, 30 minutes, Camille and Hugo, after reading the maps together.

- Commission: 8% of the price of the court, paid by the club, deducted from the payout. No fee for the player.
- Payout: every Monday, for the bookings played the week before.
- Payments: Stripe Connect, each club gets its own Stripe account through us (Camille checked with the AI: it handles the payouts and the commission for us).
- Cancellation: one rule for every club in the MVP, 24 hours. Sophie's 48 hours at peak: we'll see after the pilot.
- A slot is held 10 minutes while the player pays, like Rallyo. Not 15.
- Double booking: the club blocks the slot by hand, no sync. We'll watch it during the pilot.
- Rain: only the club cancels a slot; the player is refunded in full, and we take no commission on it.
- Split payment: not in the MVP, the player who books pays the whole court.
- The desk staff of a club: yes, a second role, who sees the bookings but not the money. Hugo: small, keep it.
- Launch the pilot on 2027-03-01, with 3 clubs: Padel Garonne, and two Hugo is talking to.
