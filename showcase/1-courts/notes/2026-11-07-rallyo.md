# A look at Rallyo (competitor)

*Fictional notes, written for the showcase of maps.md. Rallyo is an invented competitor.*

2026-11-07, Camille, one hour on the app and on the reviews.

- Covers padel, tennis, squash; lots of clubs in Bordeaux but often only a few courts of each club.
- The player pays a service fee of 1.50 € on top of the court. Many reviews complain about it ("a fee to pay for a court I could book by phone").
- The club pays 12% on top of that, paid monthly (matches Sophie).
- Split payment between players: yes, but the reviews say the booking is cancelled if one player does not pay in time. Messy.
- Matchmaking, levels, leagues, chat: a lot. The search for a court is buried under it.
- Search: sport, city, date; then a long list of clubs, the free slots of each. Good: the price is shown on each slot.
- Booking hold: the slot is held 10 minutes while you pay, with a countdown.

Takeaways for us:
- No fee for the player: the club pays the commission. Simpler price, our argument against them.
- Weekly payout: our argument for the clubs.
- Keep the search for a court first and simple. No social features in the MVP.
