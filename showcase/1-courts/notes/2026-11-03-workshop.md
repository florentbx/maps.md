# Workshop: courts marketplace, kick-off

*Fictional notes, written for the showcase of maps.md.*

2026-11-03, 2 hours, at Hugo's place. Camille (PM, writes the specs and builds the MVP with an AI), Hugo (co-founder, ran a padel club for 6 years, will sell to the clubs), Nadia (padel coach, plays 4 times a week, our "player" in the room).

## The problem, as we said it

- Players: finding a free court at a good time is a pain. You call the club during its opening hours, or you go through 3 or 4 club websites, each with its own login. Nadia: "On Sunday evening I call three clubs for a Tuesday court. The third one says yes, by phone, and I have no proof."
- Clubs: courts are empty in off-peak hours (weekdays 10:00 to 17:00), full at peak hours (18:00 to 22:00, weekends). Hugo: "An empty court at 14:00 is money lost for good, you can't sell it the next day."
- Clubs take bookings by phone and on a paper sheet or a spreadsheet; no-shows are frequent because nobody pays in advance.

## The idea

A marketplace: clubs publish their courts and their free slots, players find one and book it, and pay online at once. Name for now: **Courtly**.

- Start with padel and tennis, in Bordeaux. Hugo knows about 15 clubs there.
- Pay online at booking: no more no-shows for the clubs, a proof for the player.
- We take a commission on each booking. Hugo said 10%. Not decided who pays it: the club, or a fee added for the player.

## For whom, first

- Players who are not members of a club, or members who want a court elsewhere. Club members keep booking through their club, at the member price: we don't touch that.
- Club managers: they publish their courts, set their prices, see what's booked.

## Scope of the MVP (rough)

Must have:
- A club publishes its courts: sport, indoor or outdoor, opening hours, slot length (1 hour for tennis, 1h30 for padel), price.
- Peak and off-peak price, set by the club.
- A player searches by sport, day and time, sees the free slots, books, pays by card.
- Confirmation email, to the player and to the club.
- A player can cancel. Free cancellation until 24 hours before (Nadia: "that's what everybody does").
- The club gets its money. When? Every week? Every booking? To check.

Later, not now:
- Split the price between the 4 players of a padel court. Nadia: "everybody asks for it". Hugo: complicated, and one person pays at the club today anyway. Parked.
- Find players to complete a game (matchmaking). Big topic, later.
- Lessons with a coach. Later.
- Reviews of clubs. Hugo: clubs will hate it. Later, maybe never.
- A mobile app on the stores. A web app that works well on a phone first.

## Success

- Hugo wants 3 pilot clubs at launch, with real courts on it, not 1 court out of 8.
- Bookings paid online: we said "a few hundred in the first two months". Camille: 300 in 2 months as a target.
- Players who come back. Not said how much.

## Open points

- How does the club avoid a double booking, if it keeps taking bookings by phone? Hugo: they'll block the slot by hand. Will they?
- While a player pays, someone else could take the same slot. Hold the slot a few minutes? Nadia: "15 minutes like the train tickets".
- Rain on an outdoor court: who cancels, who is refunded?
- Launch date? Hugo: spring 2027, before the outdoor season.
- Words: we kept saying "slot", "créneau", "booking", "résa", "session", "match". To clean up.
