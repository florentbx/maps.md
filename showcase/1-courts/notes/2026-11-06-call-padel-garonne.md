# Call with Sophie, manager of Padel Garonne

*Fictional notes, written for the showcase of maps.md.*

2026-11-06, 40 minutes, by phone. Camille and Hugo. Sophie runs Padel Garonne, in Bègles: 4 indoor padel courts, 2 outdoor tennis courts, about 300 members.

## How it works today

- Members book through the club's own software, at the member price (padel: 32 € the 1h30 court, all players together). Non-members call or come to the desk: 40 € off-peak, 48 € peak.
- Off-peak, the indoor courts are empty about half the time on weekdays.
- About 1 non-member booking out of 10 is a no-show. "They book four courts in four clubs and come to one."
- The club already tried Rallyo (a competitor), left after a year: 12% commission, paid once a month. "I paid the electricity before I saw the money."

## What the club would need

- Keep the club's own software for the members. Sophie is fine blocking a slot in Courtly by hand when a member books it, "as long as it takes two clicks". No sync project.
- Set the club's own prices for peak and off-peak, per sport. Peak at Padel Garonne: weekdays from 18:00, all weekend.
- Be paid every week, with the list of the bookings.
- Under 10% commission, the club joins the pilot. "Eight, I sign today."
- Cancellation: for padel at peak hours Sophie would like 48 hours, not 24. "A Saturday 19:00 cancelled on Friday, I can't sell it again." Off-peak, 24 hours is fine.
- Outdoor tennis: when it rains, the club cancels the slot and the player must be refunded in full. The club decides, not the player.

## Other things Sophie said

- Wants to see each morning the list of the day's bookings, with the name and phone of who booked.
- Two people at the desk can manage bookings; they should have access too, without seeing the money.
- Would sign a simple contract. Does the club already have a Stripe account, for its shop? To check: Sophie was not sure.
