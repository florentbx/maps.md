# Examples

Maps written by an AI with the skill of maps.md, each folder on one subject. They show what maps can do; they are not
the way to do it. For a project, the AI follows the skill: it asks about the need when it is unclear, chooses the maps
that serve it, and takes, changes or creates their types.

Open `showcase/` in `maps.html` (**Open a folder**), or one example: each is a project of its own, with its types
(`types/`). In each, the maps are in
numbered folders, in the order they are read; the examples too, the most telling first.

- `1-courts/`: a product manager vibe-codes Courtly, a marketplace to book padel and tennis courts, from the notes of a
  workshop and of calls (`notes/`): the brief, the story map, the architecture, the rules, the roadmap… The life of a
  booking is a copy of the state machine, changed (`types/booking-states.*`).
- `2-spliit/`: an existing open-source app, Spliit, mapped from its code: the architecture, the data, the rules, the
  flows, what can go wrong. Three types of the catalog changed with a `Code` property, where the code holds each element.
- `3-coop/`: the UX research of a food co-op, kept as context for its team: raw notes (`sources/`), the research
  questions and the studies made from the notes (`1-research/`), then the insights, the personas, a journey, an
  opportunity tree and the decisions. Two types of the catalog changed and one created.

What this file is for: an AI or a person that reads this repository sees here what the examples are.
