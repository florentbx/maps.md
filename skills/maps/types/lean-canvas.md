## Context
A business idea on one page: the problem, for whom, the solution, why it wins, and how it earns money (a lean canvas).

- Each model is named after the idea it describes (`Docbook for patients`); a variant is another model.
- Write every known property on every model, with `undefined` when the context does not say.
- Problem lists the top problems, at most three, the most important first; Existing alternatives says how they are solved today.

## Description
- Kind: text
- Single: yes
- Meaning: The idea, in one sentence.

## Problem
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The top problems of the customers, at most three, and how they are solved today.

## Customer segments
- Kind: text
- Meaning: Who has the problem, and the early adopters among them.

## Unique value proposition
- Kind: text
- Meaning: Why the idea is different and worth paying attention to, in one clear message.

## Solution
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The top features, one for each problem.

## Channels
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The paths to the customers.

## Revenue streams
- Kind: text
- Meaning: How the idea earns money: the model, the price.

## Cost structure
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What it costs: to get customers, to run, to build.

## Key metrics
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The numbers that say whether the idea works.

## Unfair advantage
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What cannot easily be copied or bought.
