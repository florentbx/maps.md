## Context
The people a product is made for, by what they do: what they want, what gets in their way and how they go about it.

- A persona is a way of doing, not a person: name it by its role and what sets it apart in what it does (`The practitioner who runs late`), never by a first name.
- No age, no photo, no family, no hobby: write only what changes how they use the product. A fact about the person (age, sight, skills) goes in Context of use when the research shows that it changes what they do.
- Write what the research says; a persona drawn from no research says so in Context.
- Quotes are the words of real people, between quotation marks, never made up.
