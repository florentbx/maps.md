## Context
How a product fits a customer segment: what the customers try to do, what bothers them and what they hope for, and how the product answers each.

- Each value proposition is named after the segment it serves (`Patients who book for a relative`); another segment is another value proposition.
- Write every known property on every value proposition, with `undefined` when the context does not say.
- A pain reliever or a gain creator says which pain or gain it answers when the context does.

## Customer jobs
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What the customers try to get done: tasks, problems to solve, needs.

## Pains
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What annoys them, before, during or after a job: risks, obstacles, bad outcomes.

## Gains
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What they want or would be glad of.

## Products and services
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What the product offers this segment.

## Pain relievers
- Kind: text
- Meaning: How the product removes or reduces a pain.

## Gain creators
- Kind: text
- Meaning: How the product brings a gain.
