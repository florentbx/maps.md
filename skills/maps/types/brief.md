## Context
A product framed on one page, for the people and the AI that build it: the problem, for whom, how success is measured, what it will not do, and what it must respect.

- Each product is named as the team calls it (`Docbook for patients`); a variant or a later version is another product.
- Write every known property, with `undefined` when the context does not say.
- Non-goals are what the product will not do, even if asked, each in a few words (`No video consultations`): an AI that builds it adds nothing that one of them excludes.
- Constraints are what the product must respect: platforms, laws, budget, deadlines, tools imposed; one per value.
- A success metric says what is measured and the target (`Half of the appointments booked online within a year`); without a target in the context, what is measured alone, and the target as an open question.

## Description
- Kind: text
- Single: yes
- Meaning: The product, in one sentence.

## Problem
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The problem the product solves, and how it is handled today.

## For whom
- Kind: text
- Meaning: The people who have the problem, the first ones to serve first.

## Success metrics
- Kind: text
- Meaning: How the team will know the product works: what is measured and the target.

## Non-goals
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What the product will not do, on purpose.

## Constraints
- Kind: text
- Meaning: What the product must respect: platforms, laws, budget, deadlines, tools imposed.
